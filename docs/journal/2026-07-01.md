# 2026-07-01 — First cloud baseline + Q1 evidence (rollout, steering, AdaMPPI)

Ran the full M5 pipeline on the Lightning RTX PRO 6000 and got the first real
Q1 numbers, plus the first AdaJEPA test-time-adaptation evidence.

## Pipeline (cloud, RTX PRO 6000)

PPO walker (100 M @ 8192 envs) → 270 k diverse collect (8 parallel workers) →
DINOv3 patch precompute (20 GB cache) → JEPA train (3 epochs). Clean
convergence:

| epoch | val_pred | val_rollout | val_reg (SIGReg) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0085 | 0.0168 | 0.1433 |
| 2 | 0.0064 | 0.0135 | 0.1012 |
| 3 | **0.0058** | **0.0128** | **0.0841** |

Baseline `best.pt` (`arch_version:2`). Two Studio-env hurdles: `torch.compile`/
Triton toolchain broken → `TORCHDYNAMO_DISABLE=1` (run eager); interruptible
preemption after epoch 1 → resumed cache-warm.

## Q1a — open-loop latent rollout accuracy (the world model's true metric)

`vit-rollout`, 68 held-out episodes. **World model beats a persistence
("no-change") baseline, and the gap widens with horizon:**

- mean cosine over horizons: **0.952 (model) vs 0.905 (persistence)**
- at horizon 16: MSE **0.115 (model) vs 0.246 (persistence)** — less than half
- at horizon 1 persistence ~ties (minimal single-step change)

→ the WM genuinely captures dynamics; it is **not decorative**. First half of
"does the planner earn its keep" = yes.

## Frozen MPPI steering (17 runs, in-distribution goals)

mean `max_cos` **0.970**, mean `mean_cos` 0.835, `walk_completed` **12/17**. The
planner steers (reliably finds ~0.97-cosine latents), stable at low MPPI noise
(σ ≤ 0.02); the 5 non-completions are all σ = 0.05 (noise destabilizes the gait).

## AdaJEPA arm (AdaMPPI) — first evidence

Frozen vs test-time-adapted (predictor-only, 1 GD step/macro, per-episode
reset; DINOv3 + projector frozen), 17 paired runs:

- `mean_cos` Δ = **+0.009** (10/17 improved) · `max_cos` Δ +0.002 (near ceiling)
  · walk 12 → 12

**Weak, mixed, but thesis-consistent in direction:** the largest gains are at
**σ = 0.05** (orig ep20 +0.11, orig ep40 +0.08, diverse ep40 +0.13 / max_cos
+0.14) — i.e. exactly where the frozen model is weakest, which is AdaJEPA's
regime. Marginal overall because these goals are largely **in-distribution**
(little shift to correct). The machinery works end-to-end; the definitive test
is **true OOD goals (Q1b)** + multiple seeds, where the AdaJEPA thesis predicts
a bigger effect.

## Bugs fixed this session (all committed, 59 unit tests green)

- Q1 tooling: `vit-rollout` (rollout accuracy), `TestTimeAdapter` + `vit-eval
  --adapt` (AdaMPPI).
- Eval-path latent bugs surfaced by the first real GPU eval: goal `h5_path`
  `~`-expansion; DINOv3 fp16 → **bf16** (bf16-autocast conv clash); `_prep`
  device move (CPU pixels vs CUDA weights); adapter batch → predictor device.

## Verdict + next

The modernized world model is **sound** (Q1a) and the planner **steers** (frozen
eval). AdaJEPA gives a **small net-positive, strongest where the frozen model
struggles** — promising, not conclusive on in-distribution goals.

**Next:** (1) real OOD-goal design for Q1b (goals the walker never optimized) —
the honest test of both the planner and AdaJEPA; (2) multi-seed to separate
signal from the ±0.1 run-to-run noise; (3) Q2 reward-free diversified collect.
