## World-model search — Dreamer 4 out, TD-MPC2 rejected, LeWM in

First attempt: `nicklashansen/dreamer4`, the unofficial PyTorch port
of Dreamer 4 (Hafner 2025-09 arXiv 2509.24527). Hardware spec read

> a machine with >256 GB RAM and 8 GPUs (>24 GB of memory each)

We have one 4060 Ti × 8 GB and 31 GB RAM. Dreamer 4 is out until/unless
we rent cloud compute.

Second attempt: `nicklashansen/tdmpc2` (Hansen et al., October 2023,
arXiv 2310.16828). Hardware-appropriate — 8 GB VRAM is their stated
single-task online-RL target. Was the parallel track in [ADR 003
addendum](decisions/003-graduated-vitruvian.md). User explicitly
rejected: "TD-MPC2 is 3 years old. I absolutely do not want to use a
three year old world model. Deploy agents to research and find a
modern alternative."

Dispatched two research agents (general-purpose) to sweep Q4 2025 —
April 2026 for world-model methods that (a) are newer than TD-MPC2,
(b) target continuous control, (c) have public code, (d) fit single-
GPU single-task. Condensed finding table:

| Method | Released | Humanoid | 8 GB fit | Paradigm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LeWM | Mar 2026 | No (PushT, reacher) | ✓ (15 M params) | JEPA WM + CEM planning, offline |
| Newt | Nov 2025 | ✓ DMControl humanoid | ✓ | TD-MPC2 descendant, language-conditioned |
| WIMLE | Feb 2026 | ✓ 8/14 HumanoidBench | ✓ | Uncertainty-aware WM |
| TD-M(PC)² | Feb 2025 | ✓ 61-DoF humanoid | ✓ | TD-MPC2 + policy constraint |
| Puppeteer | May 2024 | ✓ CMU humanoid | ✗ (≥24 GB) | TD-MPC2 hierarchical |
| BeyondMimic | Aug 2025 | Real Unitree G1 | likely | Diffusion motion prior + RL |

User picked LeWM: "I love JEPA, I love LeCun." Decision recorded in
[ADR 007](decisions/007-lewm-world-model.md). Thesis alignment is
strong — JEPA as the plastic world model on top of frozen
evolutionary-equivalent priors is literally what [ADR 003](decisions/003-graduated-vitruvian.md)
proposed. Risk acknowledged: LeWM has never been applied to humanoid
locomotion, so the G1 adaptation is genuine research, weeks not hours.

## Session pivot — HWM (Hierarchical World Models) adopted as planning layer

Mid-training (full LeWM epoch running in background), user surfaced a
brand-new paper: **"Hierarchical Planning with Latent World Models"**
(arXiv 2604.03208, 3 April 2026, Zhang/Terver/Zholus/Chitnis/Sutaria/
Assran/Balestriero/Bar/Bardes/**LeCun**/Ballas, FAIR, CC BY 4.0). Two
weeks old.

The abstract motivation reads as a verbatim match for the M4.4
blockers I'd just spent 30 minutes arguing were multi-session
research:

> "learned world models often struggle with long-horizon control due
> to the accumulation of prediction errors and the exponentially
> growing search space … we address these challenges by learning
> latent world models at multiple temporal scales and performing
> hierarchical planning across these scales"

Headline: **0% → 70%** on Franka pick-and-place with goal-image-only
specification (no subgoals), using V-JEPA 2-AC as the underlying
world model. Also **3-4× less planning-time compute** on their
physics-based sim tasks (Push-T, Diverse Maze). Outperforms Octo /
π₀-FAST / π₀.₅ VLAs trained on ~77× more robotic data.

Critically: **architecture-agnostic plug-in.** The paper demonstrates
HWM on top of V-JEPA 2-AC, DINO-WM, and PLDM without modifying any of
them. LeWM is architecturally very close to DINO-WM / PLDM.

Adopted HWM as Vitruvian's planning layer for M4.4, captured in
[ADR 008](decisions/008-hwm-planning-layer.md). Key framings:

- Our trained LeWM becomes the **low-level world model in the
  hierarchy** — no wasted work.
- A new **high-level macro-action world model** gets trained on top
  of LeWM's frozen encoder, on waypoint-segmented G1 rollouts.
- Planner: top-down hierarchical CEM. High level optimizes macro-
  actions to reach a goal-image latent; first subgoal becomes
  low-level target; low level optimizes primitive 29-D actions.
- Research contribution narrows cleanly: *first application of HWM
  to humanoid locomotion + to a LeWM-style JEPA-only WM.* The paper
  only tested on manipulation and planar maze.

Roadmap updated — M4 row now references both ADRs, the
locked-decisions list has ADR 008.

### Core algorithm source: 85 lines

The hierarchical planner is in
`pldm/planning/planners/two_lvl_planner.py` and is genuinely 85 lines.
`TwoLvlPlanner(l1_planner, l2_planner, l2_step_skip)` — naming is
reversed from the paper (their `l1` is the high-level, `l2` is the
low-level); we'll adopt paper-aligned naming when we adapt.

Structure of the plan() method:

1. Encode current observation through shared encoder (`l1_planner.model.backbone(...)`)
2. Run `l2_planner.plan(current_state=backbone_output, ...)` — low-level
3. Take first predicted subgoal (`l2_result.pred_obs[1]`) as target
4. `l1_planner.reset_targets(subgoal)` — retarget the high-level
5. `l1_planner.plan(plan_size=l2_step_skip, ...)` — high-level chases

This is the research payload we're actually after. M4.4b can skip
running their full pipeline and just adapt these 85 lines + the base
`Planner.plan` method (also tiny) against LeWM's encoder.
